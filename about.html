How It All Started

Veefits was born out of a simple belief — that every woman deserves to look amazing without breaking the bank. Based in Kigali, Rwanda, we started as a small passion online business and grew into a trusted thrift destination for women across the country.

We handpick every single item in our collection. No bulk buying, no random stock — just carefully selected, quality second-hand pieces that we'd wear ourselves.

Our mission is simple: make sustainable fashion accessible, fun and completely stress-free for every resilient woman.

✨
